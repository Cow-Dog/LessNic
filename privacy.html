LessNic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

LessNic (“the app,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it’s used, and your rights. LessNic is designed as a private, offline-first nicotine-pouch reduction tool — we collect as little as possible.

1. What we collect

  • Locally on your device only: your daily pouch counts, timestamps, personal quit-message text, baseline data, and app settings. This data lives in your device’s local storage and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Apple ID identifier (when you Sign in with Apple): we receive an anonymous identifier from Apple. We do not receive your email unless you explicitly choose to share it.
  • Subscription receipts: when you purchase a subscription, Apple processes the payment and sends us a receipt identifier so we can verify your active subscription. We do not receive your credit-card information.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not run analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We do not collect your name, address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not place ads inside the app.

3. Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications — to send daily check-in reminders if you enable them. You can turn these off any time in Settings.
  • Motion / accelerometer — used only to tilt the pouches inside the dispenser visually. No motion data is stored or transmitted.

4. Data retention and deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes everything. You can also reset all data at any time from the Settings screen.

5. Children

LessNic is rated 17+ and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be communicated in the app.

7. Contact

Questions or requests (including data-deletion requests) can be sent to lessnic.support@gmail.com.
