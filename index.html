How it works

Three simple steps. The hard part is hidden inside.

No willpower required. No streaks to protect. Just a small ritual that makes "one more pouch" a little less automatic.

1

Dispense one

Before you reach for a pouch, open LessNic and dispense one. The on-screen pouch is your permission slip.

2

Take one

That small pause — opening the app, watching it dispense — is where the work happens. Don't skip it.

3

The machine learns

Over time, your daily allowance gently shrinks, calibrated to your own usage. You won't notice the math. You'll notice the change.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

If yours isn't here, email us at lessnic.support@gmail.com.

Is this medical advice?

No. LessNic is a self-help tool, not a substitute for medical care, smoking-cessation therapy, or a healthcare provider. If you're pregnant, have heart conditions, or are struggling with addiction, please talk to a doctor before changing your nicotine intake.

Why don't you show me my daily limit?

Because the moment you know the number, you optimize against it. The taper works best when you respond to your own cravings — not to a target. Trust the gradient, not the gauge.

What if I have a bad day and use way more?

The algorithm adjusts. It assumes humans are humans. There's no penalty, no streak loss, no guilt screen — just a recalibration the next morning so the path forward stays realistic.

How does the algorithm actually work?

LessNic learns your typical daily usage during a short baseline period, then gently reduces your hidden daily allowance over time. The pace adapts to how you're doing — heavier days slow the taper, lighter days let it ease forward. No fixed schedule, no rigid plan.

Does my data leave my phone?

Only the bare minimum: your Apple ID identifier (for sign-in) and your subscription status (validated with Apple). Every dispense, craving, mood entry, and stat stays local on your device. We literally cannot see your usage.

How much does it cost?

LessNic offers a 14-day free trial — no charge to start. After the trial, it's a simple monthly subscription, billed through Apple. You can cancel any time in iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. Exact pricing is shown in the app before you subscribe.

What about money-saved estimates I see in the app?

Those are estimates based on the daily pouch usage you enter, not guarantees. Actual savings depend on your real-world habits and prices. We show them as motivation, not as financial advice.

iPhone only?

For now, yes. Android is on the roadmap.

Can I delete my account?

Yes — Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This wipes your local data and signs you out. Active subscriptions are managed by Apple and need to be cancelled separately in iOS Settings.
