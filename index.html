How it works

Three simple ideas. The hard part is hidden inside.

1

Open the app first

Before you reach for a pouch, open LessNic and dispense one. The on-screen pouch is your permission slip.

2

Then take the real one

That small pause — opening the app, watching it dispense — is where the work happens. Don't skip it.

3

The machine learns

Over time, your daily allowance gently shrinks. You won't notice the math. You'll notice the change.

Questions, answered.

If yours isn't here, email us.

Is this medical advice?

No. LessNic is a self-help tool, not a substitute for medical care. If you're struggling with addiction or have health concerns, please talk to a healthcare provider.

Why don't you show me my daily limit?

Because the moment you know the number, you optimize against it. The taper works best when you respond to your own cravings — not to a target. Trust the gradient, not the gauge.

What if I have a bad day and use way more?

The algorithm adjusts. It assumes humans are humans. There's no penalty, no streak loss, no guilt screen.

Does my data leave my phone?

Only the bare minimum needed for sign-in (your Apple ID identifier) and to validate your subscription with Apple. Usage data — every dispense, every craving — stays local. Period.

How much does it cost?

30 days free, then a small monthly subscription. We'll publish exact pricing when we launch on the App Store.

iPhone only?

For now, yes. Android is on the roadmap.
